00|Meet H T T Scheduling. A full stack platform for scheduling student employees across campus, built on Next.js, a Go A P I, and PostgreSQL. Let's take a tour.
01|Everything starts with a secure sign in. Users log in with their email, or their university I D. Signing in from a new device triggers a four digit verification code. One time device verification keeps every account safe, and trusted devices skip it on the next sign in.
02|Students land on their week at a glance. George is at eight of his twenty weekly hours. Open shifts drop into the work queue, capped automatically at his maximum. One click picks a shift. Below, preferred days and times tell managers when he is free. And when life happens, requesting to miss a shift takes two clicks. Managers take it from there.
03|Now the manager's view. A new shift is added to the work queue in seconds. Time off requests wait right here. Approving one returns the shift to the queue, for someone else to pick up.
04|Schedulers own the coverage calendar. The whole week at a glance, color coded by worker. Filling a gap is one click. Pick a worker, and weekly hour caps are enforced automatically.
05|Admins run the system itself. Locations, departments, and jobs define the org, and every location gets a manager. Access control is where roles are set. Student, staff, manager, scheduler, admin. Every screen is role gated. Search anyone, change a role, or send a temporary password. And every action lands in an append only audit trail.
06|Role based dashboards, shift picking, time off workflows, audit trails, and secure authentication. H T T Scheduling keeps your campus in sync. Thanks for watching.
